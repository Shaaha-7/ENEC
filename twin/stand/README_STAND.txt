QANDEEL - stand copy (offline, no internet needed)

Start: double-click "Run Qandeel (full screen).bat"   (Alt+F4 to quit)
Keys:  1-0 sections · V views · F follow boat · G graphics level · Space pause · R restart · Esc stop tour
The guided tour starts by itself after 2 minutes without input.

Before the judges: plug the laptop in (on battery the GPU throttles to about half speed).
Windows Settings > System > Display > Graphics: set Qandeel.exe to "High performance" (NVIDIA).
Measured on the stand laptop (RTX 4050, 1920x1080): High 138 FPS, Low 175 FPS, start-up 2.8 s.
