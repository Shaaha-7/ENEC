Why forecast? Stress test model output

100 alerts tested against a "true sea" the planner did not know (currents ×0.7–1.3, wind drift ×0.5–1.7, an unmodelled 0–3 cm/s current). Arriving jellyfish that reached the mouth before protection: ~3% with Qandeel vs ~23% with a daylight camera alone (mean of 3 runs; 2.7–3.7% vs 20–26%). Median warning for crews 31–46 h.

Workboat chartered

8–10 m, bollard pull at least 1–2 t, crew of 2. Crewed and chartered. Tows a closed bag at 0.2 m/s (0.4 knots); about 7 hours to the release point 3 km NNW, because the route goes round the outer breakwater (model). Uncrewed tugs are a later step, not part of the pilot.

Ocean boom and catch pocket on the market

Desmi RO-BOOM 2000 class: 200 m length, ~66 m opening, 1.1 m skirt. Moored as a pocket at each breakwater tip, with a closed, permeable fabric bag (~20 m³ for 10 t).

Bubble curtain section add-on layer

10 × 97 m HDPE OD200 SDR17 pipes with 2 mm holes, on concrete sleepers; laid by float-and-sink like seawater pipelines. 3 L/s per metre normal, 4.5 max. Seabed depth not yet surveyed.

Air supply on the market

3 oil-free screw blowers (2 duty + 1 standby), up to 1.5 bar(g), ~87 m³/min each; Atlas Copco ZS or Aerzen Delta Hybrid class. Containerised on the western tip, fed by an 11 kV cable.

Start small

Bubble curtains move jellyfish mixed

University College Cork field and flume trials: a high-airflow curtain deflected large jellyfish; waves and small jellyfish weakened it.

Fish farms industry reports

Salmon farms using bubble curtains report 77–80% fewer jellyfish in the pens. Not peer reviewed.

Bubbles alone against

Lo (1991) found bubbles alone inadequate in a laboratory test. That is why Qandeel adds forecasting, catch pockets, boats and the existing screens.

Power-plant intakes no data

Bubble curtains have been used at intakes in Japan; no results are published. Our sea pilot would produce the first open data.

Bubble barriers at scale operating

Commercial bubble barriers run in rivers and harbours (for example plastic-catching barriers and USGS oblique screens). The hardware is proven; the jellyfish use is what we test.

What the pilot measures next

Seabed depth, local currents and waves, the depth and weight of real Blue Blubber swarms, how many the curtain holds, and the boats' working limits. ENEC shares no internal data, so the pilot creates it.
